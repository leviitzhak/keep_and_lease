Maturity-line allocation formulas


The independent previews beside the long and short controls show only the parameter-driven multiplier P(T,r). The complete raw score also contains a daily lease-edge base score, and the final weight is obtained only after normalization across that date’s available contracts.


LegContract scoreWeight

How the daily-return decomposition is calculated


Same-day reactivity executes the close-t signal at that close; next-day reactivity executes it at the following available close. Returns are then measured to the next quoted close. Weights are fractions of portfolio capital and every component is a return contribution—not a standalone asset return. The displayed components add exactly to the strategy daily return.


ComponentDaily calculationInterpretation


Silver price(wSLV + ΣwLong − ΣwShort) × (Spot[t+1] / Spot[t] − 1)The spot-silver move applied to the portfolio’s net silver exposure.


Lease carry(Σ wLong × leaseLong − Σ wShort × leaseShort) × elapsedDays / 365The lease rate observed when the position was selected, accrued linearly over the holding interval.


Rollingactual futures contribution − contribution of the previous contract mix rescaled to the same current long/short notionalsThe incremental effect of changing the contract mix. Pure resizing is excluded by comparing at the same total notionals.


Lease-rate changefutures basis contribution − lease carry − rolling, where basis contribution = actual futures contribution − (ΣwLong − ΣwShort) × spot returnThe residual repricing of futures relative to spot, including changes in the implied lease/basis while held.


TreasurywTreasury × Treasury holding-period returnReturn contributed by the cash/Treasury collateral leg.


SLV expense−wSLV × annual SLV expense ratio × elapsedDays / 365The pro-rated fund expense deducted from the SLV proxy.


Other / residualstrategy return − sum of all components aboveAn explicit reconciliation residual, retained to expose rounding or any unclassified effect rather than hiding it.




The futures contribution itself is Σ(wLong × futures return) − Σ(wShort × futures return), with each contract return calculated as F[t+1] / F[t] − 1. The strategy daily return also directly includes the SLV and Treasury contributions; NAV is then updated as NAV[t+1] = NAV[t] × (1 + strategy daily return).


For an exact product decomposition, additive contributions cᵢ to daily return R are converted to log contributions gᵢ = cᵢ × log(1+R) / R (with the continuous limit gᵢ=cᵢ at R=0). Therefore 1+R = Π exp(gᵢ). This attribution is order-independent; each displayed elementary factor return is exp(gᵢ)−1.

Parameter-only weighting preview
The heatmap colour is the signed parameter-only logit A(T,r). With the toggle enabled, it instead shows qentry(T,r) = Bentry(r)/s + A(T,r), using the configured entry rate. Softmax converts these logits into allocation weights across the contracts available on a particular date, so neither heatmap is itself a portfolio percentage.
The maturity axis extends to 10 years. In the archived curves, silver and gold observations reach about 5 years and S&P 500 observations about 2 years; the rest of the surface is an extrapolation of the configured formula.
